PORTAL-SIDE PREREQUISITE (R-P1) — this host cannot check it for you
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Until the LemonLDAP::NG portal is configured as below, ANY host enrolled in
this project can declare itself a bastion on /pam/authorize and obtain a hop
voucher (12 h by default) for a user. These are portal settings: nothing on
this host can read them back, and no command here will tell you they are
missing.

In the LLNG Manager:

  1. pamAccessServerGroups — the authoritative client_id -> server_group
     mapping. Until it is set, server_group is read from the REQUEST BODY,
     which is what lets a caller declare its own role.

  2. pamAccessAllowedRps — the RPs allowed on /pam/*. An empty list means
     "no change" for upgrade compatibility, so the upstream audience binding
     does nothing at all until you fill it in. This assumes the bastions are
     enrolled under a client_id of their own, distinct from the project's.

  3. Plugins at a published version that contains both. v0.5.2 has neither.

  4. pamAccessBastionCertPinSourceAddress — pins the source address of the
     hop certificate. Off by default.

On every backend (this one is ours, and it is the residual defence if the
four above are not in place):

  5. /etc/open-bastion/allowed_bastions non-empty:
       ob-backend-setup --allowed-bastions <bastion_id>[,<id>...]
     An empty file accepts a voucher from any bastion the SSO vouched for.

References: doc/security/09-portail-llng.md (R-P1),
doc/security/08-dossier-homologation.md (CE03, CE06, CE16, CE21).
